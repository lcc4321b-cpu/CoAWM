01 / THE IDEA

A different unit of action

Motion is a function,
not just a sequence.

Robot experience arrives through different bodies, action interfaces, and control frequencies. CoAWM represents how an action unfolds over physical time, separating the motion itself from the times at which commands are sampled.

02 / METHOD

FROM TRAJECTORIES TO ACTIONS

Two stages.
One shared space.

Learn a reusable action-function representation first, then connect it to task and visual context through a world model.

A 01

Learn the function space

A Mixture-of-Transformers encoder processes position, orientation, and gripper streams. Reconstruction and consistency across temporal views train a shared latent representation.

XYZQGTIME
B 02

Connect to the world model

Visual observations, language, predicted visual features, and robot state condition a predicted action-function latent. The pretrained encoder and decoder remain frozen.

VISIONLANGUAGESTATE
→ 03

Query and execute

A shared decoder combines the predicted latent, execution context, and physical-time queries to produce end-effector actions at the desired frequency.
